## Rationale

Regenerating derived views after record writes currently trips `validate_immutability` when `views/*.md` are tracked, while skipping regen trips `stale_views` on hook-stop. Exempting `<store>/views/*.md` is the intended fix named in the approved optimization set and unblocks the live cutover/validate loop.

## Counter-argument

Leaving views under immutability and requiring a git commit after every view regen preserves a stronger tamper story for anything under `.artifacts/`, at the cost of the known hook-stop catch-22 and continued agent friction.
